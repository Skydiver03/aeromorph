Academic Rigor

Aerodynamic Equations & Flight Test Matrix

Quantitative physics and peer-reviewed flight data from NASA Armstrong, AIAA, and AFRL.

1. NACA THIN-AIRFOIL CAMBER THEORY GLAUERT / KUTTA
$$C_L = 2\pi(\alpha - \alpha_0) + 2 \int_{0}^{\pi} \frac{dy_c}{dx} (\cos\theta - 1) \, d\theta$$

Conventional hinged flaps induce an abrupt slope discontinuity ($\frac{d^2 y_c}{dx^2} \to \infty$) creating severe adverse pressure gradients that precipitate boundary layer separation. The compliant wing dynamically increases the mean camber line smoothly ($y_c(x) \propto x^2$), maintaining attached laminar boundary layer flow.

2. PARASITIC & FORM DRAG EQUATION PRANDTL FORMULATION
$$F_D = \frac{1}{2} \rho V^2 C_D A$$

Parasitic drag scales with $V^2$. Eliminating flap cavities, tracks, and roller fairings removes cavity vortex acoustic dipole noise, directly lowering airframe approach sound by 30% to 50%.
