Aerodynamic Formulations & Compliance Equations

Analytical physics presented in class matching experimental wind tunnel and flight test validations.

Lift Dynamic Equation

Dynamic Aerodynamic Lift

$$L = \frac{1}{2} \rho V^2 S C_L$$
  • $\rho$: Air density at cruising altitude ($kg/m^3$)
  • $V$: True airspeed ($m/s$)
  • $S$: Effective wing planform area ($m^2$)
  • $C_L$: Dynamic Lift coefficient derived through active camber shape
Parasitic Drag Formulation

Skin Friction & Form Drag

$$F_D = \frac{1}{2} \rho V^2 C_D A$$
  • Parasitic drag grows with the square of velocity ($V^2$)
  • Doubling flight speed quadruples the total drag force
  • Conventional flap cavities and hinges trigger early boundary layer separation
